# Discovery and account gating

Guests may browse public discovery surfaces and eligible public listening content. Personal features—private uploads, cloud Library, Favorites, synced progress, and Remember—require a normal PlayBook account and should show a product sign-up CTA rather than a technical authentication error.

Free and Premium are subscription plans on the same account. `adsEnabled` and other capabilities are derived server-side from authentication, plan, and creator status; clients must not submit entitlement overrides.
